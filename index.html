Hi, I'm Valerio.

I'm an AI engineer and tech lead in Zurich. I build agent harness systems: the orchestration, the tools and the evaluation that an agent needs to work every time, not only in a demo. I also fine-tune models for specific domains. I use supervised fine-tuning or reinforcement learning, depending on what the task needs. And I work on foundation models for structured data. Before AI, I studied physics and did a PhD in electrical engineering at KAUST.

Working on an AI project? Agents, fine-tuning, evaluation or AI security: email me with a few lines about it.

Writing

Small experiments that take a popular claim about AI and test it. You can run each one yourself.

  • Can a decision model find where an agent failed?

    I tested Jev, a decision model, on 257 failed agent runs that people labelled by hand. On runs that fit in its window, it finds the failing step about as often as a frontier model. It cannot name the kind of failure. It knows when it is unsure.

    6 October 2026

  • Confident and wrong

    When can you trust a model that says how sure it is? I built a toy security world where the right confidence is known. Two of three training methods became honest, but only with enough data. Unfamiliar input broke all three.

    2 October 2026

  • Is visual data really richer than text?

    A picture and a list of symbols that carry exactly the same information about a toy world. The picture model learns better. Take away its convolution and the advantage is gone.

    18 August 2026

  • Nobody told it what a noun is

    A very small language model reads four novels for five minutes. Nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs separate inside it, and nothing in the training mentions grammar. With shuffled word order, they do not.
