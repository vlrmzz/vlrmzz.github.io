Work

Systems I build for clients, and my own research. One question runs through them: can you rely on what the system produced?

    I fine-tuned Qwen 32B with reinforcement learning for a multi-agent penetration-testing system, and scored it against known ground truth. The training worked, and the frontier models still moved faster. Here is when fine-tuning pays.

    Cybersecurity client

  • A multi-agent coaching system

    Tech lead on a coaching product. I contribute to its agent architecture and have shipped several of its AI coaching features. A system of several agents turns a person's raw activity data into short, specific feedback that they can act on.

    Online coaching platform

  • SDAC: assessing synthetic health data

    SEARCH is a European project for health research with synthetic data. I work on the part about trust: does a synthetic dataset protect the people behind the real data, and is it still good enough to use?

    EU project, a consortium of 26 institutions

    A model that learns from hospital records as linked tables, ordered in time, and writes synthetic patient records with the same structure.
