AI coaching from poker hand histories

A system of several AI agents that turns raw hand data into short, specific feedback that a player can act on.

Client
Online coaching platform
Role
Tech lead
Methods
Multi-agent orchestration, evaluation

The problem

A poker player produces a large number of hand histories. Solver software can already say what the correct play was in each hand. Neither tells the player what to work on.

The gap is between a correct answer and useful coaching. Which mistakes repeat? Which ones cost the most? And how do you say it so that the player changes how they play?

What I do

I am a tech lead on the product. I contribute to the agent architecture: the multi-agent system that reads the hands of a player and writes the feedback.

I have shipped several of the product's AI coaching features.
